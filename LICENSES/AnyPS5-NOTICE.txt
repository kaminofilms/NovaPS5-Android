NovaPS5 0.9.40 incorporates AnyPS5's complete AGC Vulkan renderer, shader recompiler, graphics SDK entry points and VideoOut implementation from https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5 at 9caecfd2d5a822be9daf84c69c7c33c7fafe52b9, adapted for native Android and the NovaFEX guest address space. AnyPS5 is licensed under GNU GPL version 2; see AnyPS5-LICENSE.txt. Original copyright notices remain in the corresponding source. Third-party shader dependencies retain their upstream licenses. The Android build script produces the exact corresponding source archive including the pinned dependencies and Android adapter. Nova retains its existing operating-system services, audio, input, PKG installer and CPU translation engine. This integration does not establish game compatibility or a guaranteed frame rate.
