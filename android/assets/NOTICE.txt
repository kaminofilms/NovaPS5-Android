NovaPs5 0.1.0-dev — Android development alpha

This APK does not boot or play PS5 games. The full Kyty runtime and RDNA shader
translator are not linked. Firmware files are retained without installation.

GPL-2.0-only. KytyPS5 source: https://github.com/KytyPS5/KytyPS5
The controller layout is derived from KytyPS5 snapshot df5b9b9.
Corresponding NovaPs5 source is supplied alongside this APK.
See README.md and LICENSE in the source package.
